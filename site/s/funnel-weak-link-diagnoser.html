Funnel Weak-Link Diagnoser

Use when a user funnel exists, its metrics show where users drop off, and the team is about to jump from "the number is bad" straight to a redesign without ever stating why users fall out there. Invoke it when a funnel readout shows a stage transition far below target and the proposed fixes are solutions in search of a diagnosis. Do not invoke without observed funnel numbers — build the funnel first with user-funnel-builder — and do not invoke to diagnose barriers to an offline behaviour outside a product journey; that is comb-barrier-decomposer.

The diagnostic move — competing mechanism hypotheses with discriminating tests, instead of a redesign reflex — is method, not culture, and the source playbook's cases are LMIC deployments. The hypothesis space is where WEIRD defaults creep in: explanations drawn from app-market intuitions (notification fatigue, UX friction) crowd out explanations that dominate in other contexts — airtime and data cost, shared handsets, channel trust, literacy and language mismatch. The output template's requirement that at least one hypothesis be non-psychological exists for this reason.

Inputs

  • user_funnel_map
    The staged funnel this diagnosis runs against — normally user-funnel-builder's output. Stage names, entry actions, and targets are what make a drop-off identifiable at all.
  • funnel_metric_readout
    The team's actual observed numbers for this funnel: measured value or transition rate per stage over a stated window, plus known data-quality caveats. The diagnosis targets the largest target-vs-observed gap, not the stage the team already wants to redesign.

Outputs

  • funnel_weak_link_hypotheses
    For the funnel's weakest link: competing, testable mechanism hypotheses for why users fall out at that stage, each paired with the measurement or experiment that would discriminate it from the others — not a redesign proposal.

What it does

Locates the weakest stage transition in an observed user funnel, then turns "users drop off there" into competing, testable mechanism hypotheses — each paired with the measurement or experiment that would discriminate it from the others.

When to invoke it

  • A funnel readout shows a stage far below target and the next team meeting's agenda is a redesign, a new feature, or "more onboarding messages" — a fix chosen before any mechanism was named.
  • The team can say where users leave (the numbers say so) but answers why differently depending on who's asked.
  • An engagement experiment is being planned and needs a hypothesis worth the sample — this skill's output is evidential-experiment-designer's natural input.
  • Do not invoke without observed numbers. A funnel with only targets has no weak link yet — run the product, collect a funnel_metric_readout, or build the funnel first with user-funnel-builder.
  • Do not invoke for behaviours outside a product journey (clinic attendance, savings deposits in cash). That diagnosis belongs to comb-barrier-decomposer; this skill's stage-transition framing assumes telemetry-visible steps.

What it draws on

Repeatable Motion 03 of The Agency Fund's AI Evaluation Playbook (Wu et al., 2025): use funnel drop-off data to generate "specific, testable hypotheses" about mechanism — the playbook's own examples are "Is the value proposition unclear? Are users overwhelmed? Do they mistrust the AI?" — each becoming "a lens for a focused measurement or experiment." The motion sits, in the playbook's words, at the intersection of product management and behavioural science, and its discipline is the same one a clinician applies: no treatment before a differential diagnosis, and a differential means more than one candidate mechanism held open at once.

COM-B (Michie et al., 2011) supplies the completeness check on the hypothesis space, a judgment call the playbook itself doesn't specify. Each candidate mechanism is tagged by whether it locates the failure in capability (the user can't — literacy, language, digital skill), opportunity (the context won't let them — cost, connectivity, device access, social permission), or motivation (they don't want to — unclear value, mistrust, effort not worth it). The tags exist to expose lopsidedness: a hypothesis list that is all motivation is usually a team projecting its own product anxieties, and the template requires at least one opportunity-side (structural, non-psychological) hypothesis for this reason.

Second judgment call: this skill diagnoses the largest unexplained gap, not the stage the team is most excited to fix. If the readout's data-quality caveats can explain the gap (an instrumentation change mid-window), the honest output says so and diagnoses the next-largest real gap instead.

Output template

Two to four hypotheses. One is not a differential; five means nothing gets tested. Each must be falsifiable by the test in its own row.

Known failure modes

  • The single-hypothesis diagnosis. One mechanism, stated confidently, with a test designed to confirm it. The differential is the method; if only one story was ever on the table, this skill ran as decoration for a decision already made.
  • All-motivation hypothesis lists. Every mechanism about desire and trust, none about cost, access, or capability — the classic WEIRD default, and why the template forces one structural hypothesis. Wrong locus means the winning "fix" gets A/B-tested against a barrier no message can move.
  • Diagnosing the pet stage. The team wants to rebuild onboarding, so onboarding gets diagnosed — while the numbers say the break is elsewhere. The weakest-link line must cite the readout's largest target-vs-observed gap or explicitly justify departing from it.
  • Mechanism-shaped restatements. "Users drop off because they don't engage" — a tautology wearing a hypothesis costume. The test: does the mechanism column say something the metric didn't already say?
  • Ignoring the readout's caveats. Diagnosing a gap manufactured by an instrumentation change. The gaps-set-aside line exists to force this check before farmers get theorized about.
  • Out of scope: no numbers, or no journey. Without a readout there is no weak link to find (build and instrument the funnel first); without a product journey the stage framing itself is wrong — use comb-barrier-decomposer.
